JM24 · Proof 5

Power of a Power: \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\)

Why do we multiply the exponents when taking a power of a power?

\((a^3)^2 = (a^3) \times (a^3) = a^{3+3} = a^{3 \times 2} = a^6\)

In general: \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\)

JM24 · Proof 6

Fractional Indices: \(a^{\frac{1}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a}\)

Consider \(\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^2\):

\(\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^2 = a^{\frac{1}{2} \times 2} = a^1 = a\)

Since squaring \(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\) gives \(a\), \(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\) is the principal square root \(\sqrt{a}\)!

In general: \(a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m\)
