Laws of Positive Indices

Powers mean repeated multiplication:

\(a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{n}}\) = \(a\) × \(a\) × ⋯ × \(a\)

(\(\textcolor{#facc15}{n}\) factors of \(a\))

Why \(a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n}\)

Example: \(a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}} \times a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{2}}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) · \(a\) × \(a\)

Count factors: \(\textcolor{#facc15}{3} + \textcolor{#facc15}{2} = \textcolor{#facc15}{5}\)

\[ = a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} = a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3+2}} \]
\[ a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n} \]

Why \(a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n}\)

Example: \(a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} \div a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{2}}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\(a\) × \(a\)

Cancel matching factors on top and bottom:

\[ = a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5-2}} = a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}} \]
\[ a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n} \quad (a \neq 0) \]

When the bottom power is larger

\[ y^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} \div y^{\textcolor{#facc15}{15}} \]

Larger minus smaller: \(\textcolor{#facc15}{15} - \textcolor{#facc15}{5} = \textcolor{#facc15}{10}\)

\[ = \dfrac{y^{5}}{y^{15}} = \dfrac{1}{y^{\textcolor{#facc15}{10}}} \]

Why \((a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}\)

Example: \((a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}})^{\textcolor{#facc15}{2}}\)

\(a^{3}\) × \(a^{3}\)
= \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\[ (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn} \]

Why \((ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n}\)

Example: \((ab)^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}}\)

\(a\)\(b\) · \(a\)\(b\) · \(a\)\(b\)

Regroup \(a\)'s and \(b\)'s:

\[ = a^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}} \times b^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}} \]
\[ (ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n} \]

Why \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}}\)

Example: \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\(b\) × \(b\) × \(b\)
\[ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}} \]

All Five Laws

\[ a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n} \]
\[ (ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n} \]
\[ a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n} \]
\[ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}} \]
\[ (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn} \]
