Laws of Positive Indices

Powers mean repeated multiplication:

\(a^{n}\) = \(a\) × \(a\) × ⋯ × \(a\)

(\(n\) factors of \(a\))

Why \(a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n}\)

Example: \(a^{3} \times a^{2}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) · \(a\) × \(a\)

Count factors: \(3 + 2 = 5\)

Why \(a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n}\)

= \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\[ = a^{5} = a^{3+2} \]
\[ a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n} \]

Why \(a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n}\)

Example: \(a^{5} \div a^{2}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\(a\) × \(a\)

Why \(a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n}\)

Cancel matching factors:

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) = \(a^{5-2}\)
\[ a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n} \quad (a \neq 0) \]

When the bottom power is larger

\[ y^{5} \div y^{15} \]

Larger minus smaller: \(15 - 5 = 10\)

\[ = \dfrac{y^{5}}{y^{15}} = \dfrac{1}{y^{10}} \]

Why \((a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}\)

Example: \((a^{3})^{2}\)

\(a^{3}\) × \(a^{3}\)
= \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\[ (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn} \]

Why \((ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n}\)

Example: \((ab)^{3}\)

\(a\)\(b\) · \(a\)\(b\) · \(a\)\(b\)

Regroup \(a\)'s and \(b\)'s:

\[ = a^{3} \times b^{3} \]
\[ (ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n} \]

Why \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}}\)

Example: \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{3}\)

\(a\) × \(a\) × \(a\)
\(b\) × \(b\) × \(b\)
\[ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}} \]

All Five Laws

\[ a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n} \]
\[ (ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n} \]
\[ a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n} \]
\[ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}} \]
\[ (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn} \]
