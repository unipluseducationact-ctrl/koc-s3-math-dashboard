Laws of Positive Indices

Powers mean repeated multiplication:

an = a × a × ⋯ × a

(n factors of a)

Why \(a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n}\)

Example: a3 × a2

a3
a × a2
a × a × a
a × a × a · a2
a × a × a · a × a

Count factors: 3 + 2 = 5

\[ = a^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{5}} = a^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{3+2}} \]
\[ a^{m} \times a^{n} = a^{m+n} \]

Why \(a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n}\)

Example: a5 ÷ a2

a × a × a × a × a
a × a

Cancel matching factors on top and bottom:

\[ = a^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{5-2}} = a^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{3}} \]
\[ a^{m} \div a^{n} = a^{m-n} \quad (a \neq 0) \]

When the bottom power is larger

y5 ÷ y15

Larger minus smaller:

15 − 5 = 10
= \(\dfrac{y^{5}}{y^{15}}\) = \(\dfrac{1}{y^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{10}}}\)

Why \((a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}\)

Example: (a3)2

a3 × a3
= a × a × a × a × a × a
\[ (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn} \]

Why \((ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n}\)

Example: (ab)3

ab · ab · ab
a × a × a · b × b × b
= a3 × b3

Regroup a's and b's into powers.

\[ (ab)^{n} = a^{n} b^{n} \]

Why \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}}\)

Example: (a/b)3

a × a × a
b × b × b
\[ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \dfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}} \]
