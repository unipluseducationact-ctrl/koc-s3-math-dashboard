Scientific Notation

Write a number as a × 10n where 1 ≤ a < 10.

Move the decimal to just after the first non-zero digit.

Left → 10+n.   Right → 10−n.

Large numbers — move left

Decimal left by \(n\) places \(\Rightarrow \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{n}}\)

5 0 0 0 .
\[ 5000 = 5 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{3}} \]

Example: \(234560\)

2 3 4 5 6 0 .
\[ 234560 = 2.3456 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} \]

Already has a power of 10

\[ 428.9 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} \]

Move left 2 places:

\[ = 4.289 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{2}} \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} \]
\[ = 4.289 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{7}} \]

Small numbers — move right

Decimal right by \(n\) places \(\Rightarrow \times 10^{-\textcolor{#facc15}{n}}\)

0 . 0 9 1 2 3
\[ 0.09123 = 9.123 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-2}} \]

Example: \(0.00008\)

0 . 0 0 0 0 8
\[ 0.00008 = 8 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-5}} \]

Combine negative powers

\[ 0.097 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-2}} \]
\[ = 9.7 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-2}} \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-2}} \]
\[ = 9.7 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{-4}} \]

Negative numbers

1. Ignore the minus sign.

2. Convert the magnitude.

3. Put the minus back.

\[ -428.9 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{5}} = -4.289 \times 10^{\textcolor{#facc15}{7}} \]
