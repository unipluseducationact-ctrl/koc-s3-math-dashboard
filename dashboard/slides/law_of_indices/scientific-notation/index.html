Scientific Notation

Write a number as a × 10n where 1 ≤ a < 10.

Move the decimal to just after the first non-zero digit.

Left → 10+n.   Right → 10−n.

Large numbers — move left

Decimal left by \(n\) places \(\Rightarrow \times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{n}}\)

5000
= 5000. × 100
= 500.0 × 101
= 50.00 × 102
= 5. × 103
∴ \(5000=5\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{3}}\)

Example: \(234560\)

234560
= 234560. × 100
= 23456.0 × 101
= 2345.60 × 102
= 234.560 × 103
= 23.4560 × 104
= 2.34560 × 105
∴ \(234560=2.3456\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{5}}\)

Already has a power of 10

\(428.9\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{5}}\)

∵ Move left 2 places:

428.9 × 105
42.89 × 101 × 105
4.289 × 102 × 105
∴ \(4.289\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{7}}\)

Small numbers — move right

Decimal right by \(n\) places \(\Rightarrow \times 10^{-\textcolor{#ffd54f}{n}}\)

0.09123
= 0.09123 × 100
= 0.9123 × 10−1
= 9.123 × 10−2
∴ \(0.09123=9.123\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-2}}\)

Example: \(0.00008\)

0.00008
= 0.00008 × 100
= 0.0008 × 10−1
= 0.008 × 10−2
= 0.08 × 10−3
= 0.8 × 10−4
= 8 × 10−5
∴ \(0.00008=8\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-5}}\)

Combine negative powers

\[ 0.097 \times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-2}} \]
∵ \(=9.7\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-2}}\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-2}}\)
∴ \(=9.7\times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{-4}}\)

Negative numbers

1. Ignore the minus sign.

2. Convert the magnitude.

3. Put the minus back.

\[ -428.9 \times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{5}} = -4.289 \times 10^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{7}} \]
